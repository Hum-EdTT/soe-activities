Name: Oliver

Year: 4 Learning Area: Media Arts Stream: Disability

GGoals

  1. Develop skills in creating and sharing media artworks, using digital tools and visual storytelling.
  2. Increase Oliver's ability to participate in group media projects with predictable routines.
  3. Support Oliver's sensory and emotional regulation during Media Arts activities.

SClassroom Actions / Strategies

  • Provide visual schedules and clear, step-by-step instructions for each activity.
  • Use Oliver's interests (space, marine animals, digital art) as themes for media projects.
  • Allow extra processing time and check for understanding using visual cues.
  • Offer choices between individual and small group work.
  • Prepare a quiet space or sensory tools for self-regulation.
  • Use social stories or visual scripts to support transitions and changes in routine.
  • Limit sensory overload by controlling lighting, noise, and textures in the workspace.
  • Scaffold open-ended tasks with templates, examples, and guided questions.
  • Explicitly teach and model how to interpret visual and audio codes or conventions.

Add further differentiation strategies you would apply based on your understanding of Oliver's needs.

EEvaluation

  • Monitor Oliver's engagement and participation in media projects.
  • Collect work samples and teacher observations.
  • Use checklists or rubrics for skill development.
  • Gather feedback from Oliver about his comfort and enjoyment.
  • Review progress with parents and support staff each term.

  • Model and role-play positive ways to receive and give feedback.

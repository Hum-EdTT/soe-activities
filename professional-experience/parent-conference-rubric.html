Self-Assessment Rubric

Parent-Teacher Conference

Self-assess your recorded parent-teacher conference using the rubric below. Complete this rubric once for each recording (two times total).

After recording your parent-teacher conference, use this rubric to self-assess against four criteria. For each criterion, select the level that best describes your performance. You can optionally add reflective notes. When complete, save your self-assessment as a PDF and attach it to your Padlet post. Complete this rubric once per recording — one for each of the two IEP streams you chose.

1

Role Authenticity

What worked well, and what would you do differently next time?

2

Clarity of Explanation

How clearly did you communicate the strategies?

3

Accuracy of IEP Strategies

How well did your strategies align with the IEP and stream?

4

Cultural & Context Sensitivity

Were there cultural, linguistic, or trauma considerations you handled well or could improve?

Research Skill

The Inquiry Process

A four-stage process for working through a research question — and for working back from your findings to refine it.

This activity is taller than the embedded view. For the best experience, use the Fullscreen button in the top-right corner.
Stage 1 of 4

Pose Real Question

  • 1What do I want to know about this topic?
  • 2What do I know about my question?
  • 3How do I know it?
  • 4What do I need to know?
  • 5What could be answer be?
Stage 2 of 4

Find Resources

  • 1What kinds of resources might help?
  • 2Where do I find them?
  • 3How do i know the information is valid?
  • 4Who is responsible for the info?
  • 5What other information is there?
Stage 3 of 4

Interpret Information

  • 1How is this relevant to my question?
  • 2What part support my answer
  • 3How does it relate to what else I know?
  • 4What parts do not support my answer?
  • 5Does it raise new questions?
Stage 4 of 4

Report Findings

  • 1What is my main point?
  • 2Who is my audience?
  • 3What else is important?
  • 4How does it connect?
  • 5How do I use media to express my message

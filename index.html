SOE Activities

A collection of interactive HTML activities built for the School of Education. Self-contained, embeddable components for use in an LMS or web platform across teaching and education units.

All activities are hosted via GitHub Pages and can be embedded in Blackboard, Canvas, or any platform that supports iframes. Each activity is a single HTML file with inline styles and scripts — no build step required.

Professional Experience
Activity Description Link
CMS Scenario: Managing Escalation Five-stage scenario-based learning activity following a classroom escalation. Students reflect at each stage and save their reflections as a PDF. Open
Differentiation Elements Interactive infographic showing how classroom elements (Content, Process, Product, Learning Environment) connect to student characteristics (Readiness, Interests, Learning Profile). Hover or tap any element to highlight its connecting lines. Open
Engaging with IEPs Interactive document with three pathway IEPs (EAL/D, Disability, Trauma). Students review pre-filled IEPs and add their own differentiation strategies, then save as PDF. Open
Gibbs' Reflective Cycle Interactive cycle diagram of Gibbs' six stages of reflection. Click any stage to view its description and helpful reflection questions in a modal overlay. Open
Lesson Builder — Expert Drag-and-drop lesson planning activity. Students place strategies into the correct sections of a lesson plan (Expert content level). Includes answer checking and PDF export. Open
Lesson Builder — Novice Drag-and-drop lesson planning activity. Students place strategies into the correct sections of a lesson plan (Novice content level). Includes answer checking and PDF export. Open
Parent-Teacher Conference Self-Assessment Self-assessment rubric for the recorded parent-teacher conference roleplay activity. Students score themselves against four criteria and export a PDF to attach to their Padlet post. Open
Reflective Checklist (SMART Goal Sequence) Tabbed reflective checklist across three parts (SMART Criteria, ACARA Alignment, Pedagogical Reflection). Students tick items as they confirm them and optionally save a PDF completion record. Open
Rotation Activity Review Interactive eWorksheet for reviewing the Spoons and Heat rotation stations activity. Seven sections with Yes/Somewhat/No ratings plus open responses. Save a PDF record of completed responses. Open
Teaching Strategy Review Two-column reference of 20 instructional and collaborative teaching strategies. Click any strategy to expand its description inline. Open
Understanding & Teaching Neurodivergent Students Group activity with three neurodivergent conditions (ASD, ADHD, Dyslexia). Students review provided resources and collaboratively complete four columns per condition, then export each as a PDF row. Open

Instructions

Complete each prompt below by adding your response.

For each prompt, you must respond with:

  1. An explanation of what people originally believed.
  2. What evidence led scientists to modify or reject this view.
  3. A modern example that supports today's understanding.
This activity links to ULO1: Assess current and past ideas on the prehistory of life in terms of the paradigms of evolutionary biology across species.
